## Welcome to My Blog

Welcome, and thank you for visiting my blog. The primary purpose of this space is to summarize software engineering topics for my future reference and to provide others with a concise and accessible resource for a quick glance. This blog is a place where I will document my learning journey and share insights, solutions, and techniques that I find useful.

### About Me

I have a diverse professional background spanning two industries:
- **2 years in the Software Industry**, where I developed technical solutions and built a strong foundation in programming.
- **6 years in the Banking Industry**, where I managed teams, streamlined operations, and focused on problem-solving and process improvement.

In 2022, I moved to the United States to pursue a Master’s in Computer Science. My passion for programming started in high school, and **C++** was the first programming language I learned. Over the years, I’ve continued to hone my skills and expand my understanding of software engineering principles.

### What Drives Me

I enjoy solving real-world problems, whether in programming or day-to-day scenarios. I find immense satisfaction in designing and organizing workflows, as well as optimizing solutions to make them more efficient and elegant.

Even small optimizations fascinate me—for example, ensuring a pan is centered perfectly on the burner to evenly spread heat and achieve a consistently golden-brown dosa. It’s the attention to detail that makes problem-solving so fulfilling.

### What You Can Expect

This blog will focus on:
- **Software Engineering Topics**: Summaries, explanations, and quick references.
- **Problem-Solving Approaches**: Methods and strategies for tackling challenging issues.
- **Design and Optimization**: Ideas for improving workflows and solutions in programming and beyond.

My goal is to create a resource that not only helps me but also provides value to anyone who stumbles upon this blog.

Thank you for taking the time to read this introduction. I’m excited to share my journey and learn from the feedback and insights of others. If you have any thoughts, suggestions, or questions, feel free to share them.

Stay tuned for more posts!
